# Board Slide Quality Checklist

- Every slide title is conclusion-first.
- The deck supports a concrete decision, not generic awareness.
- Executive summary fits on one slide and can be scanned in under 30 seconds.
- No slide contains a wall of text.
- Two-column layouts are used only for real comparisons or contrasts.
- Images or visuals add evidence, not decoration.
- Risks, assumptions, and next steps are explicit.
- The recommendation is unambiguous.
- The deck can be handed off cleanly to a Slides-capable execution agent.
